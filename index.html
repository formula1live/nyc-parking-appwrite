How much does it cost?

Daytime / commuter parking typically runs $20–$35, overnight $37–$60 (Midtown and FiDi can run $80–$100+), and monthly Manhattan averages $500–$1,200+. Metered street parking below 96th St is $5.00–$5.50 for the first hour, and $1.50–$2.50 in the outer boroughs. Metered parking is free on Sundays and major holidays unless a sign says otherwise.

Best neighborhoods to try

Harlem / Upper Manhattan offers the most affordable options in Manhattan proper. Financial District prices often drop on weekends. Upper East and Upper West Side garages are often more reasonable than Midtown for daily and hourly parking, especially near museums and Central Park.

Before you drive in

Check alternate side parking rules, event-night surge pricing near Times Square, Madison Square Garden and Barclays Center, and the 18.375% Manhattan parking tax (Manhattan residents may qualify for an exemption to 10.375%).
